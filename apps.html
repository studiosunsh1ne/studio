Why these apps?

There isn't one category connecting everything in this collection. That's intentional.

What connects them is the question behind them: Can a small piece of software make this particular problem easier?

Sometimes the answer is practical. Sometimes it's playful. The goal isn't to build the biggest product possible. It's to build something useful enough that someone actually wants to use it.
