The studio

Studio Sunshine is my independent app studio — a small space where ideas become real products.

I build across business, money, lifestyle, events, AI and whatever else catches my attention next.

Some ideas become full products. Some become experiments. Either way, I like seeing an idea go from “what if?” to something people can actually use.

Built from South Africa, for wherever the idea takes me.
