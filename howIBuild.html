Behind the studio

How I build.

I like making things lean. Start with an idea, build the smallest useful version, put it in front of real people and learn from what happens next.

The process
01

Idea

Everything starts with a question, a frustration, an opportunity or an idea that refuses to leave my head. The first job is figuring out whether there's actually something worth building.

02

Build

I don't try to build the perfect version first. I focus on the smallest version that can prove the idea, then improve it once there's something real to work with.

03

Launch

A product sitting on a laptop isn't a product yet. I like getting things into the real world early, even when they're still small.

04

Learn

The launch isn't the finish line. It's where the useful information starts. What people use, ignore, ask for or pay for tells me what should happen next.

What matters
⚡

Keep it lean.

I would rather launch a simple useful product than spend six months polishing an idea nobody asked for.

🎯

Solve something.

Even the fun projects should have a reason to exist. The best ideas usually start with a real human need.

🌱

Keep learning.

Every product teaches something — about people, technology, business or the idea itself.

Small doesn't
mean insignificant.

Studio Sunshine is intentionally small. That means ideas can move quickly, experiments can happen without a committee and products can evolve based on what actually happens in the real world.
