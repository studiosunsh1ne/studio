The studio

Studio Sünshine is a tiny independent app studio built around a simple idea: if something feels like it should be easier, maybe it needs an app.

I like making small products that solve small problems. Sometimes they're practical. Sometimes they're playful. Sometimes they're both.

I build from South Africa, mostly with AI, a lot of curiosity and an unreasonable number of ideas.
