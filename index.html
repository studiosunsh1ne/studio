The studio

Studio Sunshine is intentionally small. It's a place to explore ideas, build products and learn what happens when those products meet real people.

The apps don't all have to look alike or solve the same problem. What connects them is the way they're made: start with an idea, build something useful and put it into the world.

Built from South Africa, for wherever the idea takes me.
